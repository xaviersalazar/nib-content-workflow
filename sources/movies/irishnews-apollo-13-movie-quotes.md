# Apollo 13 Movie Quotes: Fiction vs. Reality

## "Failure is Not an Option"

**The Fiction:** Gene Kranz never actually said this famous line. Director Ron Howard's screenwriters invented it for the 1995 film.

**The Reality:** Kranz revealed what he truly told his flight control team:

> "I have never lost an American in space, sure as hell aren't going to lose one now. This crew is coming home."

Kranz confirmed he is "satisfied with what I said" rather than the movie version. Interestingly, he later borrowed the fictional phrase for his 2000 autobiography's title.

## "Houston, We Have a Problem"

**The Fiction:** The movie altered the astronauts' actual transmission for dramatic effect.

**The Reality:** The crew actually radioed: "Houston, we've had a problem here" after the oxygen tank explosion on April 13, 1970.

Screenwriters streamlined this to create the more memorable phrasing used in the film.

Kranz notes he constantly must correct the record, doing so "every time I speak."
